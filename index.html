Minor Project-I

🥗 Smart Nutrition Assistant

An AI-powered personalized diet and health management web project.

  • BMI calculation
  • BMR-based daily calorie estimation
  • Activity-level selection
  • Nutrition-goal selection
  • Food-preference-based meal suggestions
  • Responsive web interface

How it works

Users enter basic information and choose their activity level, nutrition goal and food preference.

The browser calculates BMI and estimates daily calorie requirements using a BMR-based formula, then displays simple meal suggestions.

Educational/demo project only. It is not medical advice and should not replace guidance from a qualified healthcare professional.
